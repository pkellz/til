
### On effective speechwriting...
**Practice**, ad nauseam.

Have a **clear & concise** message - a short, memorable quip that summarize your position.
- "Make America Great Again" - Donald Trump
- "Yes, we can" - Barack Obama
- "We choose to go to the moon!" - John F. Kennedy
- "The third way"

Have a **clear structure**
- (For example) The ["Monroe Motivated Sequence](https://www.hawaii.edu/mauispeech/pdf/mspguide.pdf)
  - Describe problem
  - How you’ll solve it
  - Visualize the solution
  - Action items

**Evoke emotions**!
- Be funny. Make people laugh.
- Use "totemic" language. Totemic words trigger certain emotional responses in people’s minds, typically by being representative or symbolic of some larger idea.
  - Sustainability ("I care about the environment")
  - Free-market system (instead of "capitalism")
  - Freedom ("I deserve / have the right to ___")
  - Progressive ("I’m forward-thinking, not a troglodyte.")
  - Pro-*choice*
  - Pay your "_fair share_"
  - Equality

**Contrast** - “not **_this_**, but **_that!_**”
- "Ask not what your country can do for you, but what you can do for your country" (John F. Kennedy's Inaugural Address)
- "Friends, Romans, Countrymen, lend me your ears. I’ve come to bury Caesar not to praise him" (_from Julius Caesar, spoken by Marc Antony_)

**3-Part List**

A speaking pattern whereby the speaker makes 3 statements, each with pauses in between, where the 3rd pause seems reasonably conclusive so the audience is inclined to clap. 

Trump 2.0 SotU comes to mind.

**Anaphora** - the repetition of a word or phrase at the beginning of successive clauses

“**_A man dies_** when he refuses to stand up for that which is right.

**_A man dies_** when he refuses to stand up for justice.

**_A man dies_** when he refuses to stand up for that which is true” - Martin Luther King Jr.

**Epiphora** - the repetition of a word or phrase at the end of successive clauses

“Long as the white man sends you to Korea, **_you bled_**.

He sent you to Germany, **_you bled_**.

He sent you to the South Pacific... **_you bled_**.” - Malcolm X

**Framing**
- Talk about the future
- Talk about the group / nation
- Individuals
- Values
    - Freedom
    - Liberty
    - Brotherhood
- Themselves (I’m one of you)
